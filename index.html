ABOUT MUSKAN HELP CARE

A Compassionate Mission for
Human Dignity & Social Upliftment

"Dream to achieve more for the social cause." — Official Motto, Muskan Help Care (Regd. Office: Sangam Vihar, New Delhi)

Muskan Help Care is a registered voluntary organisation established to bridge critical gaps in society. As documented in our Annual Activities Report 2025-26, our foundation operates on the firm belief that no child should go hungry, no family should freeze in the winter cold, and no woman should remain deprived of independent livelihood opportunities.

While institutions adopt decentralized approaches across the country, local pockets still suffer deplorable conditions in both infrastructure availability and economy. Muskan Help Care works directly on these frontlines:

No Royalties, Pure Service

Whatever comes to the Society by way of contribution is utilized 100% for the development of its humanitarian programmes, free of personal gain.

Social Awareness Through Art & Books

Promoting civic consciousness, literacy, and environmental care through books, educational drives, and collaborative literature.

Empowerment & Self-Reliance

Equipping women with vocational tailoring & cutting training to transform dependants into confident, earning breadwinners.

Rapid Disaster & Seasonal Relief

Swift on-ground deployment during floods, emergency medical crises, and biting winter cold with blankets & mosquito nets.

Our Key Welfare Programs
Serving Grassroots Communities

Directly from our verified List of Activities in Brief, Muskan Help Care runs targeted initiatives addressing hunger, disaster relief, health, education, and women empowerment.

Program #01

Ration Distribution Prog.

Providing comprehensive dry ration packages (flour, rice, pulses, cooking oil, spices, and salt) to underprivileged families, daily wage laborers, and widows facing food insecurity.

  • Monthly staple grocery hampers
  • Direct doorstep distribution
  • Prioritizing single-mother households
15,000+ Kits
Program #02

Environment Awareness Prog.

Engaging communities in tree plantation drives, plastic reduction seminars, clean water conservation, and neighborhood hygiene awareness to protect our environment for future generations.

  • Neighborhood sapling plantations
  • Anti-single-use-plastic workshops
  • Waste segregation education
4,500+ Saplings
Program #03

Help For Poor

Direct emergency interventions for individuals under deplorable economic conditions. Includes emergency medical bill assistance, clothing drives, destitute welfare, and elderly support.

  • Emergency medical & surgery aid
  • Clean clothes & footwear distribution
  • Destitute & elderly care packages
Direct Aid
Program #04

Nutrition Distribution Camp

Specialized nutritional support camps organized in slum clusters to combat child malnutrition and maternal anemia with enriched meals, fresh fruits, eggs, milk, and protein supplements.

  • Children & expectant mothers focus
  • Pediatric nutrition screening
  • Vitamin & calcium supplement packs
8,000+ Meals
Program #05

Flood Relief Camp

Rapid response deployment in disaster-affected riverine and low-lying inundation zones. Supplying clean drinking water, dry rations, first-aid kits, tarpaulins, and temporary shelters.

  • Safe drinking water purification
  • Ready-to-eat dry ration hampers
  • Water-borne illness prevention
25+ Camps
Program #06

Books Distribution Prog.

Empowering poor students with the power of literacy. Providing curriculum textbooks, notebooks, school bags, geometry boxes, and educational writing to ensure no student drops out.

  • Free NCERT & syllabus books
  • Stationery kits & school backpacks
  • Art forms & social awareness writing
8,500+ Students
Program #07

Blanket & Mosquito Net Prog.

Protecting street dwellers, elderly residents, and slum inhabitants from harsh northern winters with warm blankets, and providing durable mosquito nets to prevent dengue & malaria outbreaks.

  • Heavyweight winter blankets
  • Medicated mosquito net distribution
  • Night drives for pavement dwellers
12,000+ Units
Program #08

Health Awareness Prog.

Organizing free health check-up camps, blood pressure and sugar screening, dental hygiene awareness, adolescent health counseling, and essential non-prescription medicine distribution.

  • Free doctor consultation camps
  • Basic medicines & hygiene packs
  • Preventive wellness counseling
6,800+ Checkups
Program #09

Tailoring & Cutting Prog.

Our flagship women livelihood initiative. Providing hands-on vocational courses in sewing, garment cutting, dress designing, and stitching machine usage to help women earn respect and self-sufficiency.

  • Free training & course materials
  • Sewing machines facilitation
  • Micro-enterprise financial literacy
3,200+ Women

OUR CORE DISTINCTION

Why Stand With
Muskan Help Care?

Trust is the cornerstone of every social organization. Here is why philanthropists, volunteers, and local communities believe in our grassroots mission.

100% Non-Profit Operation (No Royalties)

As declared in our official report, whatever comes to the Society by way of contribution is utilized directly for program development. No royalty is given to authors or members.

Decentralized Grassroots Presence

Rather than confining ourselves to offices, our team is rooted in Sangam Vihar and deeply underserved colonies where basic infrastructure and economy are deplorably inadequate.

Immediate Relief + Lifelong Skill Building

We do not just provide temporary relief like food or blankets; our Tailoring & Cutting training programs empower beneficiaries with self-earning capabilities for lifelong dignity.

Registered Voluntary Status & Transparent Audits

Fully compliant with legal governance as a registered voluntary organization with regular annual activity reports published and documented.
